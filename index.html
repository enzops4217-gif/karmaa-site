Questions

How do I optimize Windows 11 for gaming?

Run the Scan: 22 checks (Game Mode, power plan, services, graphics driver, display…) show what slows your PC down. Then apply a profile or the tweaks one by one, and compare with the Before / After test bench. Everything undoes in one click.

Do my settings stay after a restart?

Yes. They are saved in Windows and stay in place. If Windows, an update or other software undoes one, the app sees it at the next startup, restores it on its own and tells you which one.

How do I get more FPS on PC?

Start with what holds it back the most: power plan, Game Mode, background programs, an up-to-date graphics driver and the right refresh rate. The Scan checks these and fixes them in one click; for Call of Duty and Fortnite, the app also tunes the game’s own options.

How do I reduce input lag on Windows 11?

Turn on NVIDIA Reflex in your games, turn off V-Sync, use a high-performance power plan and a mouse at 1000 Hz or more. KarmaaOptimizer also measures driver latency core by core to find the ones that cause micro-stutters.

Does it work with Call of Duty and Fortnite?

Yes. The app has a section for each, plus Apex Legends, CS2 and Rocket League: optimized settings in one click, a preview before writing and a backup. Every other game benefits from the Windows tweaks.

Do I need a powerful PC?

No. The app works on any PC running Windows 10 or 11. Windows tweaks help most when the processor or background programs limit your FPS, which happens on recent PCs too.

Will it get me banned by anti-cheat?

No. The app changes Windows settings and never injects anything into your games.

Can I undo the changes?

Yes. All 181 tweaks can be undone in one click, and nothing is hidden.

Which Windows versions work?

Windows 10 and Windows 11.
