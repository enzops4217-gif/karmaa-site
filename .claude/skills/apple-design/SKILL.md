# Apple design

Source: [VoltAgent/awesome-design-md](https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-design-md) — `design-md/apple/DESIGN.md` (MIT, see `LICENSE`).

Before writing any UI, read `DESIGN.md` in this folder and follow its tokens and rules:
- one interactive accent (Action Blue `#0066cc`, `#2997ff` on dark),
- full-bleed tiles alternating light / parchment / near-black, with the color change as the divider,
- SF Pro (system-ui) or Inter, headlines at weight 600 with tight tracking, body at 17px,
- pill CTAs, `scale(.95)` press state, no decorative gradients or glows, a single shadow reserved for product imagery,
- frosted (backdrop-blur) sticky nav.
