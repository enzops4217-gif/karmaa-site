How the conversion works

Each game turns your mouse movement into rotation with its own multiplier. The converter keeps the distance your mouse travels for a full turn (cm/360) identical, so your muscle memory carries over from one game to another.

What is a good cm/360?

  • 25 to 35 cm: common for tactical shooters (CS2, Valorant), where precise flicks matter more than fast turns.
  • 18 to 30 cm: common for fast games with high mobility (Apex, Call of Duty, Fortnite).

There is no perfect value. Pick one you can aim with consistently and keep it in every game.

What about aiming down sights?

This tool converts hip-fire sensitivity. When you aim down sights, each game applies its own multipliers. Keep them on the game’s default or “relative” setting for the most consistent feel.

Questions

Is DPI or in-game sensitivity better?

What matters is the product of both (eDPI) and your cm/360. A DPI of 800 or 1600 with a lower in-game sensitivity is the most common choice.

Does field of view change the conversion?

Not for hip-fire turning: the distance for a full 360° stays the same. It changes how fast targets seem to move on screen.

Why is my Fortnite value in %?

Fortnite shows its X/Y sensitivity as a percentage. Enter and read it as a percentage.
